Agent Adoption Program

Your team has the tools. Are they using them?

Most companies buy agentic coding tools faster than their engineers adopt them. A handful of people use them well, most try once and go back to old habits, and by renewal nobody can point to what changed. The problem isn't the tool. It's that nobody owned the rollout.We do.

01

Adoption Assessment

2 weeks

We work with your engineering leadership to establish where you actually are: who's using what, where the time goes, which work is a good fit for agents and which isn't, and what's blocking the engineers who've stalled. You get a written assessment with a prioritized rollout plan and a baseline you can measure against.

02

Pilot Program

6 weeks

We run a structured pilot with one team on real work, typically the maintenance and migration tasks agents handle best. Hands-on working sessions, not slideware. We establish the workflows that fit how your team actually ships, and measure the difference against the baseline.

Quoted per engagement
03

Team Enablement

ongoing

Once the pilot works, we help it spread: training sessions for additional teams, internal champions, documented practices your organization owns, and a measurement rhythm so adoption stays visible to leadership.

Why us

We run DevKick, the builder community in the Waterloo to Toronto corridor, a monthly room of engineers shipping production work with coding agents. We see what actually works before it reaches the blog posts, and we bring that into your organization.

We work deepest with Devin, and our method is tool-agnostic: the assessment, the pilot structure, and the measurement work regardless of which agent platform you've standardized on.
