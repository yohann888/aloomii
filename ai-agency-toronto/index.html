What we do

AI consulting with a delivery path.

Buying an agent is easy. Changing how a team works is the hard part. Our engagements connect strategy to a real rollout, with a baseline, hands-on working sessions, and practices your organization can own.

Adoption assessment

Establish where your team is, who is using what, which work is a fit, and what is blocking the people who stalled. You receive a written assessment and prioritized rollout plan.

Pilot program

Run a structured pilot with one team on real work, typically maintenance and migration tasks agents handle well. Working sessions replace slideware.

Team enablement

Spread what works through training sessions, internal champions, documented practices, and a measurement rhythm that keeps adoption visible to leadership.

Relevant experience

Built by operators who have shipped in serious environments.

The team brings production software experience from JP Morgan, IBM, the Ontario Government, and Maersk. Aloomii also runs DevKick, a builder community in the Waterloo to Toronto corridor, where engineers share what they are shipping with AI agents.
