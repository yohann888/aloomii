Why community

Software is table stakes. Community is the moat.

In a world where AI can build almost anything, the one thing it cannot generate is a room full of the right people who trust each other. We build both: the systems that scale, and the relationships that last.

We don't pitch guests or run your outreach. You tape the show, we make it worth watching.

Not a marketplace freelancer

We've produced five shows, a $4B launch broadcast, and the systems behind them. This isn't a gig.

Not a course
